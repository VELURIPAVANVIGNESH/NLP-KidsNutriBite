Why was NutriKid built?

Generic conversational LLMs treat numerical clinical thresholds as stochastic language predictions. In pediatric nutrition, conflating 30g of protein with 30mg or applying adult EAR values carries severe clinical consequences.

Deterministic Architecture

What makes NutriKid structurally different?

Five interlocking architectural constraints prevent stochastic drift, ensure unit fidelity, and tie every assertion to audited page references.

01

Hard Age Boundary & Refusal Gate

Unlike generic conversational systems that extrapolate infant formulas or adult supplements, NutriKid strictly enforces 5.0 ≤ age ≤ 15.0 yrs. Out-of-bounds queries trigger an immutable refusal signal before vector retrieval initiates.

Gating Precision: 100% Deterministic AST
02

Hybrid Retrieval (BM25 + FAISS Dense)

Captures exact clinical acronyms (e.g., "RDA", "EAR", "IFCT") through lexical Okapi BM25 alongside dense semantic embeddings (bge-large-en-v1.5) fused via Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF, k=60).

RRF Factor: k=60 Hybrid Indexing
03

Specialized Food Table Parsing

Flattening PDFs destroys numerical multi-column alignment. NutriKid utilizes schema-aware table extractors preserving all 8,351 Indian Food Composition Tables (IFCT) rows with intact nutrient column headers.

Rows Preserved: 8,351 IFCT Matrix
04

Neural Cross-Encoder Reranking

Initial top-50 vector matches are passed to BAAI/bge-reranker-base for intensive query-passage cross-attention, boosting Mean Reciprocal Rank by +244.4% compared to standard bi-encoder retrievals.

MRR Lift: +244.4% Cross-Encoder
05

Nutrient Fidelity Unit Guard

A rule-based validation layer inspects generated text before render. If protein is stated in micrograms or calcium in grams, the output is flagged or regenerated to prevent catastrophic medical dosing guidance.

Unit Matrix Verified Pre-Flight AST

Every clinical synthesis must link to at least one valid canonical chunk ID with exact page coordinates. Syntheses lacking provenance are discarded immediately.

What NutriKid DOES

  • done Retrieves verifiable, age-specific dietary recommendations from audited institutional sources.
  • done Calculates cohort-level RDA/EAR thresholds based on published reference weights.
  • done Provides deterministic chunk-level source citations and PDF coordinates for instant human audit.
  • done Refuses out-of-scope age queries (under 5.0 or over 15.0 years) to protect pediatric safety.
  • done Executes on local LLM runtimes (Qwen2.5-1.5B) to guarantee zero third-party telemetry of clinical queries.
cancel

What NutriKid DOES NOT Do

  • close Does NOT diagnose medical conditions, acute malnutrition, or pediatric metabolic disorders.
  • close Does NOT prescribe patient-specific therapeutic diets, parenteral nutrition, or insulin-to-carb ratios.
  • close Does NOT serve infants (<5.0y) or adults (>15.0y) requiring specialized clinical protocols.
  • close Does NOT substitute for direct examination and judgment by a licensed medical practitioner.
  • close Does NOT promote branded nutritional supplements, commercial infant milk foods, or unverified wellness trends.
